pmsa is a work-hour registration app. Developers log begin/end hours against projects, switching between several projects on a single working day. "Elevated" users manage projects and people and correct others' entries. Reporting must be viewable from multiple angles (per user, per project, per timespan) and includes project hour budgets with burn-down.

**Why:** It replaces a very old-school in-house tool that does plain input with almost no follow-up beyond a single simple hours report. The pain is not data entry — it is that nothing useful comes back out of the data.

**How to apply:** When scoping or building pmsa features, bias effort toward the *follow-up* side (week review, corrections, charts, burn-down) rather than polishing the entry form. Entry should be fast and forgettable; insight is the differentiator. The story map lives at `docs/product/story-map.md` (17 stories across 4 activities: Log my time, Review & fix my week, Manage projects/people/access, Report & visualize).
